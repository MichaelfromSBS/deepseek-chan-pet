# Release 1.0.0

The first public release contains the approved artwork revision 3.

- Resting, left and right movement, and review animations preserve the approved character.
- Waving includes gentle body sway and hair follow-through.
- Jumping is a low hop with a small knee bend and soft landing.
- The setback state is a relaxed seated rice break with bowl and chopsticks.
- Waiting for input shows a seated begging pose with an empty bowl.
- Active work shows a seated pose with a laptop on her lap.

The final local atlas passed the native structural validator and the ChatGPT Pets preflight. The stored ChatGPT Pets atlas was downloaded and verified to have identical decoded RGBA pixels; its encoding differs from the local WebP.

On the original workstation, earlier concepts, builds, review reports, rejected drafts, and backups remain under `archive/`. That local history is excluded from the public repository. The installed local pet remains in the Codex home’s `pets/deepseek-chan` folder.
